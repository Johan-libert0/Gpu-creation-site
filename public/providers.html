Choose work on your terms

Accept only the jobs you choose.

Providers can review the prompt-job terms and accept or decline work. In this integration, actual model inference runs through Hugging Face Inference Providers, not on the accepting provider's own GPU.

CONTROL

Inspect every term

Review the prompt-job title, model processing, and proposed payout before accepting a request.

EXECUTION

Managed inference

The deployment's Hugging Face token calls the configured Inference Providers model. Model access, availability, pricing, and data handling depend on Hugging Face and the selected inference provider.

ACCOUNTABILITY

Recorded outcomes

Derived output, job status, approval, and settlement state remain separate, auditable records rather than automatic promises.
